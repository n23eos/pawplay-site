PawPlay bot privacy

Updated: 11 October 2026

Scope

This policy applies to @pawplay_purchase_bot. The extension continues to keep pet names, watch progress and game coins in the local Chrome profile. It does not send that extension data to the bot.

Data received

Telegram sends the bot your user and chat ID, language and incoming commands or messages. Updates can also contain your Telegram name and username. We temporarily queue updates until processing completes, then remove them from that queue. For orders we retain user ID, language, order ID, price, currency, consent and purchase timestamps, payment identifier, payment/refund status and delivery status. We do not receive your card details or Telegram password.

Purpose and storage

Order records are needed to verify payments, deliver and recover keys, handle refunds, prevent duplicate charges and resolve payment disputes. Order records and queued updates are encrypted on the bot server, with the encryption key stored separately. Access is restricted to the operator. The bot does not use analytics or sell customer data.

Retention and deletion

We keep purchase records while needed for key recovery, refunds, disputes or applicable obligations. An unaccepted quote is replaced when you start a new purchase. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion of your information. We remove data no longer needed; information that must be retained for a payment dispute or legal obligation may be kept for that purpose. Deleting the purchase record can prevent future key recovery through /key.

Support and external services

Telegram handles messages and Stars payments under its own privacy policy. The support form is hosted by Google Forms; its contents, including your optional reply email and purchase details, are received by Google and the developer. They are used to investigate your request and reply when you ask for a response. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Public GitHub issues are for non-sensitive bug reports; use the private form for payment information.

Contact

The operator is the PawPlay developer, GitHub account n23eos. Use the private support form below for privacy or purchase requests. Never submit passwords, activation keys or bot tokens.
