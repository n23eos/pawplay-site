PawPlay Plus purchase terms

Updated: 11 October 2026

Seller and product

PawPlay is sold by its independent developer, GitHub account n23eos. Google, YouTube, Telegram and Ko-fi are not the seller. Plus adds ten pets and five progress bar styles to the free extension. It does not sell game coins or guarantee future features.

One-time purchase

In @pawplay_purchase_bot the price is 150 Telegram Stars, shown again before payment. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Stars top-up prices vary by client and payment method. A Ko-fi purchase has the price displayed at its own checkout; it is a separate purchase channel.

Delivery and activation

After Telegram confirms payment, the bot sends the activation key as text in the same private chat. Copy it into the PawPlay extension activation field. Keep it for reinstalling or another Chrome profile. Use /key in the same Telegram account to recover your purchase. Delivery can be delayed by Telegram or network outages; contact support if the key does not arrive.

Use of the key

The key unlocks the current Plus collection for personal use. Do not resell or publish it. Activation works offline using a shared key, rather than a personal account. A refund stops repeat delivery through the bot, but cannot individually revoke a key already issued. A purchase does not guarantee that the extension or this bot will remain available forever.

Refunds

Request a full refund within 14 days after purchase, without needing to give a reason. For a missing or unusable key or a duplicate charge, also contact support after that period. Mandatory rights under applicable law remain unaffected. Refunds are processed manually to the original payment channel: Stars for a Telegram purchase, or the original Ko-fi payment method. Processing depends on the payment platform. We do not request a transfer fee to issue a refund.

Payment support

Use /paysupport for the private support form. Include PawPlay, your Telegram username or user ID, purchase date and the problem. For a Ko-fi purchase, use its purchase reference. Never include an activation key, bot token, password or card details, and do not post payment identifiers in public GitHub issues. Support uses the form to investigate and arrange a refund or help with delivery.

Consent and changes

Read these terms before pressing the acceptance button in the bot. Changes apply to future purchases and do not reduce rights for an already paid order. These terms describe PawPlay purchases; Telegram and Ko-fi also apply their own platform terms.
