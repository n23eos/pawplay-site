PawPlay purchase support

Updated: 11 October 2026

Private payment help

For a missing key, duplicate payment, refund or privacy request, use the Google Forms support form below. Choose Bug report, mention PawPlay purchase support in the description, and describe what happened. This existing form also accepts refund and privacy requests. Include your Telegram username or user ID and purchase date so the developer can locate the order. Add a reply email if you want a response. The form is an existing developer support channel.

Keep secrets private

Do not include activation keys, bot tokens, passwords or card details. Do not put payment identifiers in public GitHub issues. For a Telegram purchase, /key can resend a purchased key and /paysupport opens this support page.
